# Manual release verification kit

This is an executable protocol, not completed evidence. Record real observations and attach failures to a repair branch before release sign-off. Use the fixed Greenwich demonstration to avoid collecting participants' birth details.

## Evidence record

For every session record the exact application commit/release-manifest hash, OS, browser/version, screen size, zoom, input method and relevant assistive technology or physical graphics adapter. Record pass, fail, blocked or not tested for each task, the observed behavior, and the issue/repair PR for failures. Do not leave a blank field that could be read as a pass.

## Screen reader and keyboard

Use an actual screen reader (for example NVDA with desktop Firefox/Chrome, or VoiceOver with actual Safari on macOS). Record its version and mode. An accessibility-tree inspection or axe run is supporting evidence only.

1. Start at the welcome screen. Find the fictional example and birth entry, and identify the example after opening it.
2. Reach the object list using the skip link, select Moon, read its sign/house/horizon and interpretation/source. Use Find to select Taurus and a reference angle. Confirm the selection is announced once and the inspector content is reachable.
3. Open/close the inspector and explorer, pin/clear selection, and repeat at 200% browser zoom/narrow desktop width. Focus must remain visible and meaningful when a drawer closes.
4. Change house system and all three displays. Read an undefined polar result and confirm bodies remain available. Operate a construction stage/sweep and hear a useful value/name.
5. Inspect an aspect and reach its measured longitude separation, tolerance, interpretation and source. Confirm color/glyph meaning has a textual counterpart.
6. Play, pause, step and scrub the timeline. Confirm fields retain normal arrow/space behavior and playback does not announce every frame. Return to the birth instant and restore the original context.
7. Enter a DST fold/gap, explicitly choose an occurrence, cancel the dialog and confirm focus returns. Simulate lookup outage and use manual coordinates/offset. Open/close the evidence dialog.
8. Enable a trail, change its frame and read sampling/limit states. Trigger a recoverable asset or worker failure and use Retry. Note any silent error, repetitive announcement or keyboard trap.
9. Focus a body, aspect, layer and each new feature category. Confirm that the passive preview is described by the focused control, preserves selection and dismisses with Escape. Reach a catalogue star, construction element, trail sample, historical cusp, projected house and individual geographic line through the bounded navigators. Repeat after changing the trail frame or house method; a stale sample must never acquire a current body interpretation.
10. With a mouse, dwell over an aspect strand, cross another target on the way into its card, scroll/copy the reading and press Escape. Confirm both actual placements, their equal visual weight, the aspect symbol and measurements. Check all five themes, sparkles On/Off, reduced motion, Low quality and forced colours. Read at 200% actual browser zoom and large text with both panels open.
11. Independently toggle constellation figures, official regions and catalogue stars. Identify the figure as a drawing convention and the region as an unequal sky area. Select a star and a figure with keyboard and canvas routes.
12. Re-enter Earth overview, then Outside sphere and Observer sky. Confirm the close observer-facing globe and explicit geographic-layer presets, without changing the chart. Override a child layer, change time and focus a geographic line; the override must remain. Compare a sky house with its projected section, inspect a geographic crossing and read the body/angle explanation. Test an undefined polar partition without losing valid geographic lines. Advance time, lose/restore the graphics context and verify the exact current camera pose.

## Actual browser/device checks

Record actual Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari versions tested. The proposed current/previous-major support range is unverified for any version not listed. Playwright WebKit is an engine test and does not establish actual Safari/macOS support.

On each browser inspect the 63 ordinary combinations (seven systems × three displays × three viewpoints), plus polar/provisional and recovery states. Check clipping, transparent surfaces, labels/leaders, overlap choice, pointer drag/cancel, resize/zoom, reduced motion, focus and context recovery. Compare calculated values rather than requiring identical antialiasing across platforms.

For a physical reference GPU, record the adapter/driver, hardware acceleration and actual WebGL renderer. Measure default, selected construction, comparison and one-body trail interactions; report delivered frame intervals, long input stalls and GPU measurements when available. Include the default globe with houses/all forty geographic lines, figures and the dense sky combination. Measure picking and card reveal after its intentional dwell, compare sparkle On/Off with matched camera input, and verify idle rendering and bounded resources. Separate CPU submission time from completed GPU time. Repeat context restoration after advancing time in default Earth overview as well as after a manual orbit. A SwiftShader run cannot satisfy the physical-device gate.

## Moderated comprehension evaluation

Recruit approximately five visual learners and two people experienced with astrology. Record participant category without collecting birth information or personality claims. Explain that the evaluation concerns understanding the interface, not belief or predictive validity. Give each task without demonstrating its solution; record whether help was needed, first interpretation, completion, errors and the participant's explanation.

| Task prompt | Observe and ask after the attempt |
| --- | --- |
| Enter the provided fixed moment, then explore another observer location. | Can they distinguish moving the observer from rotating the camera, and identify which birth context remains fixed? |
| Compare two house systems at that moment and find a changed assignment. | Do they understand that boundaries/classification changed while geocentric body directions did not? |
| Find the same house in arcs, volumes and shells. | Can they recognise membership, follow boundaries, understand depth and select the same entity in all modes? Which view helps which question? |
| Select an aspect and explain the relationship shown. | Can they identify both bodies, separate longitude measurement from the curved strand, read the orb and reach tradition/source labels? |
| Explore a short and long interval, then show a trail in each reference frame. | Can they state time direction/scale, distinguish stars versus horizon and describe retrograde as apparent motion? |
| Inspect a below-horizon object and an undefined construction. | Can they explain each state and identify the absence of a substitute house calculation? |

After each task ask: “What does this represent? What changes it? Where does its interpretation come from?” Record the answer before offering an explanation. Capture recurring misunderstandings as actionable findings, implement fixes through the established branch/review process, and retest the affected task. Do not manufacture participants, scores, quotes or a successful evaluation.

## Human editorial review

The plan requires a human check that each cited passage supports its claim. The current catalogue's review metadata records engineering/agent curation; it is not a completed human-editor session. Review the 320 concept/placement entries, including all 120 body-in-sign and 120 body-in-house readings and the fifteen geographic overview/body/angle additions, against their specific cited passages. Review the combined aspect card as a deterministic presentation of three registered claims, without inferring an additional pair-specific prediction. Record the entry ID, reviewer/date, source passage, attribution/tradition, supported or unsupported result, and any repair PR. Keep astronomy, definitions, tradition and evidence roles distinct. A general overview lacking that placement or an inaccessible source must not receive a false support pass. Recheck any changed text and its combined source-page word budget.

## Sign-off

Link actual evidence and any repair PRs in the final release report. Unperformed screen-reader, actual Safari, integrated reference-GPU, participant or human editorial sessions remain open gates. Engineering completion and a deployable artifact can be reported separately from full acceptance.
