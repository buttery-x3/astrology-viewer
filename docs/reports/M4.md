# M4 — House displays and comparison

Implemented on `codex/m4-house-displays`, based on the reviewed M3 development integration. Main remains unchanged.

## Delivered

- Seven calculation systems and three independent house rendering choices: volumes/wedges, boundary arcs, and layered shells. Each consumes the same indexed longitude intervals and fixed-J2000 geometry. Clockwise polar partitions retain house identity; geometry never sorts their cusps into a different rule.
- Adjustable fill, contextual fill, selected volume hybrid, and selected shell separation with leaders and an original outline. House numbers and cusp labels are distinct from sign glyphs. Ecliptic-pole ambiguity is excluded from hit testing.
- Twenty-four stable selectable identities (`house:1`–`house:12`, `boundary:1`–`boundary:12`) with canvas/list selection, hover, explicit focus, indexed interval inspector and linked body assignments. Body selection highlights its primary house without moving the marker.
- Atomic primary/comparison calculations. Requested and displayed system labels remain separate while pending or failed. Ghost boundaries are dashed and changed-only by default; an exact union of both cusp sets identifies changed regions and assignment links. Body inspectors show both systems' actual results.
- Undefined or degenerate partitions hide only their overlay and explain the state. No substituted cusps or house numbers are installed. A valid secondary result is labelled separately rather than presented as the primary system.
- Renderer objects and typed buffers are reused across modes and ordinary chart updates. Capacity growth disposes the previous geometry; scene teardown owns all resources.

## Verification

- 165 tests pass, including numerical fixtures, all 21 system/mode combinations, actual reverse polar C/R cases, outward/nondegenerate closed volume meshes, boundary anchors, off-ecliptic membership, exact cusps and wrap, pole hit limits, comparison partitions, atomic response ordering, buffer reuse/disposal, shell leaders and undefined overlays.
- Fourteen production Chromium workflows pass. M4 adds all 21 selectable system/display combinations, fixed geocentric positions, stable membership, actual focused-house canvas picking, comparison assignment links, and polar failure with retained bodies/angles.
- Type checking, lint, production build and asset integrity checks pass. Independent reviews of state/UI and renderer integration found no unresolved blockers. Review corrections removed shell-lift claims when a boundary or undefined house had no lifted shell.
- Desktop visual checks cover the three modes, lifted shell/original leaders, ghost comparison, and observer/outside destinations. Production compressed application code/data is about 583 kB, separate from worker/WASM/ephemeris files; full device/network budgets remain M8–M9 work.

## Convention boundary

The full-sky extension of each longitude interval is a display convention. It does not claim that every system is constructed from these meridians or volumes. M5 supplies separate, numerically validated actual constructions, particularly the time-based Placidus and Koch definitions.
