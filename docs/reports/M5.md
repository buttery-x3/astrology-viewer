# M5 — Actual house constructions

Implemented on `codex/m5-house-constructions`, based on reviewed M4 development. Main remains unchanged.

## Delivered

- Per-system independent reconstruction and three interactive stages for the selected indexed cusp. Whole Sign/Equal use their longitude anchors, Porphyry uses directed ecliptic quadrants, Regiomontanus/Campanus project their actual reference divisions through the horizon's north/south axis, Placidus uses each cusp's own constant-declination semi-arcs, and Koch uses MC-degree timing and past/future shifted horizons.
- A guide sweep illustrates the defining rule while the actual chart, observer, positions and cusps remain fixed. Validation always reports the final construction. Sidereal angle/hour units and held-horizon timing references are explicitly distinguished from civil time and actual motion against the stars.
- Selected-cusp marker, actual defining reference/projection/timing curves, sourced short explanations, method-specific numeric timing, final/native residuals and polar-convention notes. The live legend uses the renderer's role metadata and only visible construction roles/stages.
- Only validated guides enter the renderer. Undefined, singular or mismatched constructions withhold geometry and explain why. No equal-wedge substitute represents a temporal method.
- Guide hover identifies its actual reference; focus and canvas picking preserve the indexed boundary. Comparison lines quiet while the guide is active. Pooled objects/typed buffers and a shared line-buffer helper preserve resource reuse and teardown.

## Numerical evidence

The committed native fixture has **92 observer/instant cases and 644 system results**: 564 defined and 80 undefined, including 16 reversed polar results. It covers both hemispheres, equator, ±89.9°, normal/near-polar dates at the 1900/2100 boundaries, four orientations, and the date's polar-circle latitude at ±1e−5° and ±1e−8°.

All defined results reconstruct every cusp within the required 0.01°. The largest measured residual is approximately **0.000002545° for Placidus**; other method maxima are below 7e−12° in this fixture. Native/engine validity agrees; failed Placidus/Koch results remain undefined. Signed projection preserves Regiomontanus/Campanus polar branches rather than choosing the nearest antipode. See [the contract and reproducible fixture procedure](../house-construction-contract.md).
