# M6 — Aspects and sourced inspector

Implemented on `codex/m6-aspects-inspector`, based on reviewed M5 development. Main remains unchanged.

## Delivered

- Five major longitude aspects have categorical colours, symbols and dash patterns, pixel-width cores and optional restrained glow. Selected-body connections are emphasized; selecting a pair isolates its strand. Conjunctions retain a nonzero bracket, and selected opposition endpoint indicators remain inspectable when Earth obscures a body.
- The connector is a display route. Its measurement is a separate ecliptic arc with longitude anchors and latitude-projection leaders. The inspector distinguishes longitude separation from the actual three-dimensional sky angle, and reports target, departure, configured orb and exact eligibility.
- Explicit ±6° defaults are editable independently for each type. Filters and appearance controls do not recalculate or move bodies. A stable selected pair retains its numerical inspector after moving outside its orb, without a stale active strand.
- A versioned, fixed catalogue contains 305 cards: 120 body-in-sign, 120 body-in-house and 65 concepts. All have reviewed original summaries, claim-level roles and exact source passages, authors/publishers, named traditions and reuse notes. Astronomy, method, project convention, traditional interpretation and evidence are displayed separately.
- Body cards resolve the calculated sign and house. Provisional time qualifies the reading; undefined houses withhold house interpretations while retaining body/sign content. A central evidence explanation reports the specific scope of the cited controlled study and separates calculated geometry from interpretive validity.
- CI validates full coverage, IDs, role/source agreement, review status, combined canonical-page word budgets and checked-in manifest hashes. Existing M3/M5/provenance claims sharing those sources are included. Content is never fetched or generated at runtime.

## Verification and review

315 unit/numerical tests pass, including 22 aspect renderer/curve tests and 16 catalogue/domain tests. Eighteen production Chromium workflows pass; after the final geometry corrections, the five affected aspect/house workflows were repeated. Type checking, lint, production build and content validation pass. All 91 canonical source pages remain within their editorial budgets, including shared M3/M5/provenance prose.

Root and independent agents reviewed the numerical geometry, controls, inspector and attribution. Review found and corrected a reversed measurement-arc delta, missing closeness opacity encoding, duplicate house headings and source scopes that conflated project display choices with native calculation rules. Actual scene views were inspected for selected pairs, longitude anchors, observer focus, conjunction overlap and opposition handling.

Timeline/trails, comprehensive accessibility/performance and release validation remain M7–M9.
