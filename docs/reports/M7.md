# M7 — Timeline and motion history

Implemented on `codex/m7-timeline-trails`, based on reviewed M6 development. Main remains unchanged.

## Delivered

- A uniform UTC scrubber, window presets and zoom preserve the current moment independently of the immutable session birth anchor. The timeline distinguishes requested and calculated instants and shows the birth marker or an off-window indicator. Top context identifies birth versus exploration and the active house system.
- Forward/reverse playback integrates a monotonic clock into exact chart requests. It pauses on hidden tabs, context editing, calculation errors, sustained backpressure, clock interruptions and range boundaries. No uncalculated extrapolation supplies scene positions or classifications.
- Minute/hour/day steps use elapsed time. Month/year steps use explicitly labelled Gregorian UTC calendar arithmetic, clamping invalid end-of-month dates while preserving clock time. Window lengths are labelled as fixed durations; a day step is 24 hours and may change local clock time at DST.
- A separate, cancellable worker samples one selected body. Fixed J2000 directions express motion relative to stars; independently sampled ENU directions are re-embedded in the current horizon for local-horizon motion. Each projection inserts the latest exact body direction without restarting the job on every chart tick.
- Adaptive sampling combines midpoint angular error and maximum time gaps. Bounded batches yield to cancellation and other work. Point/time/range limits preserve only the verified sampled interval, reporting limitations rather than joining uncalculated gaps. Cache identity includes the target, frame, observer, interval, sampling policy and pinned calculation versions.
- Past strokes fade with age; future strokes are dashed. Direction arrows, sampled UTC ticks, start/end labels and a live reference legend explain the path. One selected indexed boundary has at most seven separately labelled historical arcs. Whole Sign changes are discrete and undefined samples omit geometry.

365 unit/numerical tests pass, including 30 trail sampling/service tests, 14 trail renderer/path tests and six timeline tests. The 23 production Chromium workflows pass across the full suite and affected reruns. Type checking, lint, production build and existing content gates pass. Checks cover native Moon samples in both frames, exact current insertion, near-polar/reversed and undefined history, Whole Sign jumps, bounded cancellation, strict worker responses, cache/retry/disposal and buffer reuse. At most 1,999 precomputed points reserve a slot for the latest exact current sample, keeping displayed trails at or below 2,000 vertices.

Root and independent agents reviewed transport, domain, worker/service, renderer and UI integration. Actual scene views at 1440×900 and 1024×800 show both frames, complete UTC captions, honest long-window limits and seven independent boundary ghosts without page errors or footer overlap. Real pointer tests allow CSS-pixel picking precision; inverse coordinate fixtures retain their tight numerical tolerances. Rendered history is a sequence of sampled boundaries, not a claim to identify every event between samples.

Comprehensive accessibility/performance, operational packaging and final release validation remain M8–M9.
